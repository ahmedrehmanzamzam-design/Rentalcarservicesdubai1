From selecting the right vehicle to checking total costs, documents, insurance and return arrangements, use this guide to plan your Dubai car rental with fewer surprises.

1. Choose a Vehicle That Fits Your Trip

Start with the reason you need a car. An economy hatchback can work well for solo travel and everyday errands. A sedan may suit business appointments or a small family, while an SUV can offer more room for passengers and luggage. Luxury vehicles can suit special occasions, but their rental and running costs are generally higher.

Economy car

Practical for city journeys, short stays and budget-conscious travel.

Consider one when extra passenger or luggage space matters.

Premium car

Compare the full rate and conditions for special occasions.

Think about where you will park, how much luggage you have, how many people will travel and how far you expect to drive. The biggest vehicle is not always the most convenient choice in busy areas.

2. Compare the Full Rental Cost

Rental prices can vary by model, dates, rental duration, availability and included services. A headline rate may not represent the final amount, so request a written breakdown before confirming the booking.

  • Rental period: compare daily, weekly and monthly rates for your dates.
  • Deposit: confirm the amount, payment method, release process and expected timeline.
  • Insurance: ask what is included, the excess amount and exclusions.
  • Mileage: check the permitted distance and extra-kilometre fee.
  • Additional charges: ask about Salik tolls, fuel, parking, delivery, late returns and administration fees.

A monthly rental may lower the average daily cost for a longer stay, depending on the vehicle and agreement. Check extension, cancellation and early-return conditions before committing.

3. Prepare the Required Documents

Documentation depends on whether you are a UAE resident or visitor, your nationality and licence type, the rental company's rules and the vehicle category. UAE residents generally need a valid UAE driving licence. Visitors need a licence accepted for driving in the UAE and may need an International Driving Permit depending on their circumstances.

Ask the provider to confirm the exact documents for your situation before paying. Check the minimum driver age, accepted payment methods, deposit conditions and whether additional drivers must be listed on the agreement. For common booking questions, visit our car rental FAQs.

4. Plan for Roads, Salik and Parking

Plan your route before setting off, allow extra time during busy periods, obey posted speed limits and avoid using a handheld phone while driving. Parking rules and payment methods differ by area, so read signs carefully and use designated spaces.

Dubai uses the Salik electronic toll system. Ask how tolls are recorded and billed for your rental and whether an administration fee applies. Confirm the fuel-return policy too, so you know whether the car must be returned with the same fuel level or under another agreed arrangement.

5. Confirm Collection and Return Details

Handover arrangements affect how convenient your rental will be. Confirm the full address, time, parking or meeting instructions, and the number to call if you are delayed. If delivery or return at another location is available, ask whether an extra fee applies.

6. Inspect the Car Before You Leave

Take a few minutes to document the vehicle's condition before driving away. Follow the rental company's inspection process and save the records with your booking confirmation.

  • Photograph visible scratches, dents, glass damage and wheel marks.
  • Check lights, mirrors, seat belts and air conditioning.
  • Record the fuel level and mileage on the dashboard.
  • Confirm you have the relevant vehicle documents and emergency contact details.
  • Report concerns before departure and retain a record of the response.

At return, follow the agreed handover process, remove personal belongings and keep confirmation that the vehicle has been returned. Ask how any deposit release will be handled.

7. Choose a Rental Company With Clear Terms

Good service involves more than a low price. Look for a clear written agreement, responsive communication, vehicle-condition records and transparent explanations of additional charges. Customer reviews can provide useful context, but read several detailed reviews rather than relying on one comment.

Before booking, review the company's cancellation and refund policy and keep a copy of the agreed terms. You can also read customer testimonials for additional context; individual experiences may differ.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is renting a car in Dubai useful for tourists?

It can help visitors planning multiple stops, travelling with family or luggage, or staying where public transport is less convenient. For a few journeys on metro-connected routes, taxis or public transport may cost less.

Is daily or monthly rental better?

Daily rental can suit a short visit, while a monthly package may offer a lower average daily rate for longer stays. Compare total cost, mileage, insurance, deposit and extension conditions.

What extra charges should I ask about?

Ask about the deposit, insurance excess, extra kilometres, Salik tolls, fuel, parking, delivery, late return and administration fees. Request a written explanation of what is included.

Can all visitors drive a rental car in Dubai?

Visitors must meet applicable UAE driving and rental requirements. An International Driving Permit may be required depending on nationality and licence type, so confirm your circumstances before booking.

What should I do before returning the car?

Return the vehicle at the confirmed time and location, follow the agreed fuel policy, remove personal items and document the handover. Ask for return confirmation and details of the deposit-release process.
